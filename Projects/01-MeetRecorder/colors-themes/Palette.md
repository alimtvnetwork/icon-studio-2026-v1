## Usage
- **Primary gradient** (`Primary` → `PrimaryGlow`) for the icon body and brand surfaces.
- **Foreground (white)** for the M letterform on dark/purple surfaces.
- **Accent (teal)** reserved exclusively for the record indicator and active recording states.
- **Background** for app chrome; **SurfaceDark** for elevated panels.
